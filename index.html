The Indigenous Heritage

Born from Ancient Nigerian Soil, Revered Worldwide

Long before industrial refining stripped oils of their soul, West Africa celebrated the miraculous fruit of the Elaeis guineensis palm.

In the fertile coastal and rain-belt forests of Southern Nigeria, the oil palm grows under intense tropical sunshine and rich loam soil. The fleshy orange-red mesocarp of this sacred fruit yields a thick, luscious unrefined oil with a biological concentration of natural carotenes (provitamin A) that exceeds carrots by fifteen times.

At BTC Oils, our philosophy is anchored in Better Taste & Care. We honor this ancient legacy by preserving the raw vitality of the palm fruit without bleaching, deodorizing, or adding harmful synthetic colorants.

The Journey of the Ruby Drop

How we transform fresh bunches of Elaeis guineensis into pristine, laboratory-clean virgin red palm oil without losing a single nutrient.

Phase 01 // Harvest

Hand-Selected Peak Ripeness

Only fully mature palm fruit bunches with deep maroon caps and vibrant orange bases are hand-harvested by local Nigerian smallholders. Fruits showing bruising or over-fermentation are discarded immediately to ensure our signature ultra-low acidity.

01
02
Phase 02 // Steam Softening

Gentle Steam Conditioning

Instead of boiling in open wood drums that create burnt odors, BTC uses controlled low-temperature culinary steam. This inactivates lipolytic enzymes while protecting heat-sensitive tocotrienols and provitamin A carotenoids.

Phase 03 // Virgin Extraction

Hygienic First-Press Cold Extraction

The softened pulp is mechanically pressed using food-grade stainless steel presses. No chemical solvents like hexane are ever used. What flows out is pure, unadulterated virgin palm nectar with an intoxicating, fresh nutty aroma.

03
04
Phase 04 // Micro-Clarification

Natural Multi-Stage Filtration

The oil passes through physical micro-mesh filters that separate moisture and natural fiber sediments without stripping the vibrant ruby-red pigments. The result is crystal-clear oil that stays smooth, stable, and sludge-free.

Phase 05 // Airtight Seal

Sealed Tamper-Proof Packaging

Sealed into food-grade, heavy-duty gallons and certified 20L, 25L & 30L commercial yellow jerrycans. Every batch is stamped with lot tracking and direct contact details, delivering uncompromised quality from depot to kitchen.
